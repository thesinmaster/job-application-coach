# After applying

## Application tracker

Keep this table in the conversation and update it whenever the user reports news. Offer it as a spreadsheet if they have many applications.

| Company | Role | Applied | Channel | CV version sent | Contact | Status | Next action | By |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

"CV version sent" matters: every later profile change and every interview answer must stay consistent with it.

## Follow-up timing

| Situation | Wait | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Applied through a portal, no reply | 2 to 3 weeks (longer for large multinationals) | One short email or message to the recruiter: still interested, asking whether the application is under review |
| After an interview | 5 to 7 working days, or one day after the date they gave | One short message thanking them and asking about next steps |
| Sent to an insider contact | Do not chase the contact | Apply through the official channel too |
| No reply after one follow-up | - | Stop. Keep the contact warm for future roles |

Large employers are slow; three weeks of silence is normal and not a rejection. Do not write applications off too early, and do not chase twice.

## Reading signals

- **Posting reposted** with a new date: the first pool did not convert, or the hirer refreshed a paid slot. Either way it is the only change that justifies one re-approach after a rejection.
- **Posting text or title changed**: the role was redefined. Re-run the fit check.
- **"Promoted by hirer"** alone: a paid placement, not a signal.
- **Headline and email title differ from the body**: the smaller description is usually the real job.
- **Rejection that gives a reason** (level, compensation band, location): the most valuable data in the search. Use it to adjust targets, not to argue.

## Replies

### Rejection: gracious close and feedback request
In the language the recruiter used. Short. Thank them, ask for one piece of feedback, keep the door open. Then connect on a professional network such as LinkedIn a day or two later with a one-line note, not on the same day.

> Dear [Name],
> Thank you for letting me know, and for keeping me informed throughout the process. If you are able to share any feedback on where my profile fell short against the criteria, I would value it for future applications. Please keep me in mind if a similar role opens in your team.
> Best regards,

### Rejection with a reason, then the posting is reposted
See "Re-approaching after a rejection" in `cv-and-cover-letter.md`. Once only.

### Positive reply
- Confirm the slot quickly.
- Re-read the posting, the fit report and the CV version sent.
- Prepare with `interview-prep.md`, including the candidate's own questions.
- After the interview: a short thank-you within 24 hours, in the interview's language.

### Offer
List the package elements (base, bonus, car, insurance, leave, notice, probation, location rules). Compare against the floor set at intake. For contracts, tax status or non-standard clauses, recommend a local lawyer or accountant before signing.
