# Intake questions

Ask these once, early, and in the user's language. Do not send the whole list at once: start with the CV and the first three questions per role, then go deeper on the roles that matter for the target posting.

## For each role (most recent first)

1. Company, title, dates, city. Was the title the same as what you actually did? If not, what would describe the job better?
2. Scope: how many people reported to you (directly and in total), how many sites or locations, what budget, volume or revenue you were responsible for.
3. **Built or ran?** What did you set up from nothing, what did you inherit and improve, and what did you only operate?
4. The two or three results you are proudest of. For each: the situation before, what you did, the result. Is there a number? If you do not remember it, say so - we will not invent one.
5. Projects with people outside your own team: other departments, other countries, suppliers, consultants, IT teams. Who led them?
6. Anything regulated or audited: safety, quality, compliance, customs, data protection, financial controls. Any audit results?
7. Systems and tools you used daily, and which ones you implemented or configured yourself.
8. Why did you leave? (Answer as you would to a recruiter. We will refine it.)

## Across the career

- Education and certifications, with years. Anything in progress?
- Languages and real level (could you run a meeting in it?).
- Any gap longer than three months, and what you did during it.
- Things you did outside the job that show skill: publications, side projects, volunteering, teaching, board roles.
- The kind of role you want next, and the kind you would not take.

## Constraints

- Current city. Would you commute (how long, how often)? Relocate (where)? Travel (how much)?
- Salary floor and target. Gross or net in your country? Employee or contractor?
- Notice period or availability date.
- Other applications currently open, and on which platforms.
- Which job platforms you have a profile on (professional networks, job boards, company career portals), and whether any of them still holds an older CV.

## Follow-up questions while writing

When a line in the CV is vague ("responsible for warehouse operations"), ask the question that turns it into a result:
- How big was it?
- What was broken when you started?
- What changed because of you, and how do you know?
- What would your manager at the time say you were best at?
